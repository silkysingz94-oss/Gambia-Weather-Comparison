Methodology and limitations

1. The national boundary defines the analysis extent.

2. A regular latitude/longitude sampling grid is generated, excluding points outside the boundary.

3. Weather data are requested for each valid sampling point.

4. Forecast and ERA5 hourly means are calculated independently from valid observations.

5. Difference is calculated as Forecast minus ERA5.

6. These are gridded weather values, not direct ground-station observations.
